## 1. 🏛️ Brand Foundation

### Our Mission
Verdant exists to make sustainable living effortless — crafting eco-friendly home products that prove you never have to choose between what's good for your home and what's good for the planet.

### Our Vision
A world where every household is a force for positive environmental change — one thoughtfully made product at a time.

### Brand Story
Verdant was born out of a simple frustration: why did choosing sustainable home products always mean compromising on quality, aesthetics, or convenience? Our founders — two product designers obsessed with both good design and environmental responsibility — set out to change that.

We spent two years sourcing materials, testing formulations, and partnering with ethical manufacturers before putting a single product on a shelf. The result is a line of home essentials that look beautiful, perform exceptionally, and leave the world a little better than they found it.

Today, Verdant products are in thousands of homes across North America. Our customers aren't just buyers — they're a community of people who believe that the everyday choices we make add up to something meaningful. We're here to make those choices easier, and a lot more beautiful.

### Core Values
| Value | What It Means to Us |
|-------|----------------------|
| **Sustainability** | Every material, process, and packaging decision is made with the planet in mind — no greenwashing, no shortcuts |
| **Transparency** | We tell you exactly what's in our products, where it comes from, and how it's made |
| **Craftsmanship** | Sustainable doesn't mean disposable — we make things to last |
| **Accessibility** | Going green shouldn't be a privilege; we keep our products beautifully priced |
| **Community** | Our customers are partners in a larger mission, not just transactions |

### Brand Personality
We are: **Fresh**, **Genuine**, **Thoughtful**, **Confident**, **Warm**

Think of Verdant as a person: the knowledgeable friend who genuinely loves the planet, always has a recommendation that actually works, never makes you feel guilty for not being perfect, and has effortlessly great taste. They're the one whose home always feels calm and considered — and who'll happily tell you where they got everything.

## 2. 🗣️ Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice vs. Tone
- **Voice** is who we are — warm, knowledgeable, genuine. This never changes.
- **Tone** shifts: we're celebratory at a product launch, calm and helpful when a customer has an issue.

### Our Voice Pillars

| Pillar | Description | Do | Don't |
|--------|-------------|-----|-------|
| **Warm & Genuine** | We sound like a real person, not a corporation | "We care about this too — here's why it matters to us." | "Verdant is committed to stakeholder sustainability initiatives." |
| **Knowledgeable Without Lecturing** | Share expertise; never shame | "Beeswax wraps can replace up to 1,000 plastic bags — here's how to get started." | "You're still using plastic bags?!" |
| **Optimistic & Motivating** | Focus on what's possible, not what's failing | "Small changes at home add up to something real." | "Plastic is destroying the planet and most people don't care." |

### Tone by Context
| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Marketing copy | Inspiring, sensory-rich | "Home should feel good — for you, and for the world." |
| Customer support | Warm, solution-focused | "We're sorry to hear that — let's fix it right now." |
| Social media | Conversational, curious, celebratory | "Your compost bin update just made our week. 🌱" |
| Error messages | Calm, helpful | "Something didn't go through — give it another try, or reach us at hello@verdant.co" |
| Milestones | Joyful, grateful, grounded | "1 million plastic bags replaced. This one's yours as much as ours." |

### Writing Style Rules
- Use active voice — "We plant a tree" not "A tree is planted"
- Write conversationally — read it aloud; if it sounds stiff, simplify it
- Keep sentences under 20 words in marketing copy
- Use "you" and "we" freely — this is a relationship
- Avoid guilt, fear, or shame as motivators — lead with hope and possibility
- Prefer specific over vague — "made from organic cotton" beats "made sustainably"

### Words We Love
Fresh, craft, made with care, intentional, real, everyday, together, thoughtful, clean, restore, local, grow, plant, honest

### Words We Avoid
Revolutionary, disruptive, synergy, leverage, game-changing, natural (vague — be specific), eco-friendly (overused — say exactly what we mean), solution, stakeholders

## 3. 🎨 Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
The Verdant logo consists of a simplified leaf mark paired with the wordmark "Verdant" set in our primary typeface. The leaf mark is formed from a single continuous line that curves into a leaf shape, symbolizing organic flow and simplicity. The wordmark uses slightly tracked letterforms for a clean, confident presence.

### Logo Variants
| Variant | When to Use |
|---------|-------------|
| Primary (Forest Green wordmark + mark) | Light backgrounds, primary marketing materials |
| Reversed (Cream/White) | Photography overlays, dark packaging backgrounds |
| Monochrome (Black) | Single-color applications, embossing, engraving |
| Mark Only (leaf icon) | App icon, favicon, social media profile images, small emboss on products |

### Clear Space
Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "V" in the wordmark on all four sides. Never crowd the logo with imagery, text, or competing graphic elements.

### Minimum Size
- **Digital:** No smaller than 120px wide (full logo); 32px (mark only)
- **Print:** No smaller than 30mm wide (full logo); 10mm (mark only)

### Logo Do's ✅
- Always use on approved backgrounds (cream, white, forest green, dark photography)
- Scale proportionally — always lock aspect ratio
- Use the mark-only variant at very small sizes

### Logo Don'ts ❌
- Do not stretch, compress, or distort the logo
- Do not add drop shadows, glows, or bevels
- Do not change logo colors to anything outside the brand palette
- Do not place on busy, clashing, or low-contrast backgrounds
- Do not recreate or redraw the logo freehand
- Do not rotate the logo

All Verdant text/background combinations must meet **WCAG AA** standards:
- Deep Charcoal `#2C2C27` on Cream `#FAF7F0` — ratio ~14:1 ✅
- Cream `#FAF7F0` on Forest Green `#2D6A4F` — ratio ~7.5:1 ✅
- Warm Stone on Cream — borderline; only use for UI labels at 16px+ ⚠️

## 5. ✍️ Typography

### Font Families

#### Primary Typeface — Lora
Used for: Headlines, display text, pull quotes, hero sections  
Style: Humanist serif with contemporary sensibility  
Source: Google Fonts (free)  
Why: Lora feels warm, trustworthy, and literary — perfectly aligned with Verdant's thoughtful, genuine personality. The subtle ink-trap details feel handcrafted without feeling rustic.

**Weights used:**
- Regular (400) – subheadings, pull quotes
- Bold (700) – primary headlines

#### Secondary Typeface — DM Sans
Used for: Body copy, navigation, UI labels, captions, button text  
Style: Geometric humanist sans-serif  
Source: Google Fonts (free)  
Why: DM Sans pairs beautifully with Lora — it's clean and modern, giving the system a fresh, approachable quality that prevents the brand from feeling too traditional.

**Weights used:**
- Regular (400) – body copy, descriptions
- Medium (500) – UI labels, navigation
- Bold (700) – callouts, emphasis, buttons

#### Accent Typeface — Lora Italic
Used for: Pull quotes, special brand moments, testimonials  
No third typeface required — italic Lora provides sufficient accent personality.

---

### Type Scale (Desktop)

| Element | Font | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|---------|------|------|--------|-------------|
| H1 / Hero | Lora | 56px | Bold | 1.15 |
| H2 / Section Title | Lora | 40px | Bold | 1.2 |
| H3 / Subsection | Lora | 28px | Regular | 1.25 |
| H4 / Card Title | DM Sans | 20px | Medium | 1.3 |
| Body Large | DM Sans | 18px | Regular | 1.65 |
| Body Default | DM Sans | 16px | Regular | 1.65 |
| Caption / Label | DM Sans | 13px | Medium | 1.4 |
| Button Text | DM Sans | 15px | Bold | 1.0 |

### Typography Do's ✅
- Always lead with Lora for emotional, brand-forward moments
- Use DM Sans for functional, instructional, or UI copy
- Maintain hierarchy — the reader's eye should flow naturally from large to small
- Use Lora Italic sparingly for emphasis in long-form content

### Typography Don'ts ❌
- Never use more than these two typefaces in a single layout
- Never set body copy below 14px
- Avoid centered text for body paragraphs — left-align for readability
- Never use ALL CAPS for body copy — use sentence case throughout
- Never stretch or condense letterforms — use the correct weight instead

## 6. 📷 Imagery & Photography Style

### Art Direction Principles
Verdant photography feels like a calm Sunday morning — natural light, real moments, beautiful imperfection. Nothing should look overly staged or artificially perfect. The visual world is warm, green, textured, and full of life.

### Subject Matter
- **People:** Real, diverse humans in real home settings. Candid, not posed. Laughter over perfection. Ages 25–45 primarily, but inclusive across demographics.
- **Settings:** Kitchens, living rooms, gardens, farmers markets, morning routines. Natural environments where appropriate.
- **Products:** In use in context — not sterile product-on-white shots (reserve those for e-commerce). Show the product living in a real home.

### Photography Do's ✅
- Use natural lighting — soft, directional, warm (golden hour tones)
- Feature real textures — wood, linen, clay, stone, plants
- Include greenery and natural elements in the frame
- Capture authentic moments — a messy but beautiful kitchen is perfect
- Warm color temperature: around 5,000K–6,500K
- High resolution: minimum 3,000px on shortest side for editorial; 2,000px for digital

### Photography Don'ts ❌
- No harsh overhead studio lighting — creates an antiseptic feel
- No heavy filters, heavy vignettes, or extreme saturation boosts
- No perfectly symmetrical, overly designed scenes — we're real, not IKEA
- No stock photo clichés (smiling white family around a table, generic hands holding product)
- Avoid cold or blue-tinted images

### Image Overlay & Text Treatment
When placing text over images:
- Apply Forest Green overlay at 50–65% opacity, OR use a soft black overlay at 40%
- Ensure body text contrast meets WCAG AA
- Always use Cream or White text on dark overlays

## 7. 🔷 Iconography & Illustration

### Icon Style
- **Style:** Thin line icons with soft rounded caps
- **Stroke weight:** 1.5px at 24px base
- **Corner style:** Rounded
- **Grid:** 24px baseline, with 32px and 48px variants
- **Color:** Forest Green primary, Sage for secondary icons, Warm Stone for disabled/inactive states

### Illustration Style
Verdant uses minimal botanical illustrations as accent elements — single-line drawings of leaves, plants, and natural forms. These appear in packaging, editorial layouts, and brand stationery. They should feel hand-drawn but clean.

### Guidelines
- Never mix the line icon style with filled or 3D icons
- Botanical illustration elements should only appear in Forest Green, Sage, or Cream
- Icons must be consistent in visual weight across the entire product

## 8. 💻 Digital Applications

### Website
- **Primary background:** Cream `#FAF7F0`
- **Section breaks:** Sage `#74A57F` at 10% opacity or solid Forest Green `#2D6A4F`

## 9. 🖨️ Print & Packaging

### Business Cards
- Size: 3.5" × 2"
- Front: Forest Green background, Cream Verdant logo centered
- Back: Cream background, Deep Charcoal text — name, title, email, website
- Font: DM Sans 10pt for contact details
- Finish: Soft-touch matte laminate with Forest Green foil on the logo mark

### Letterhead
- Verdant logo top-left in Forest Green
- Thin Forest Green rule beneath header (1pt)
- Body: DM Sans 11pt, Deep Charcoal, 1.5 line height
- Footer: Sage rule + address + website in DM Sans 9pt, Warm Stone

### Product Packaging
- Primary packaging color: Cream with Forest Green typography
- Botanical illustration accents on all packaging surfaces
- All packaging materials: recycled, FSC-certified, or compostable
- Minimum logo size on packaging: 15mm mark width
- Required: ingredient/material list, origin, certification marks (B Corp, compostable, etc.) in DM Sans 9pt

## 10. 🚫 Brand Don'ts

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Instead |
|----------|-----------|
| Use fonts outside Lora + DM Sans | Stick to the approved type system |
| Use colors outside the approved palette | Reference the official color guide |
| Stretch or distort the logo | Lock aspect ratio — always |
| Use guilt-based or fear-based language | Lead with possibility and optimism |
| Say "eco-friendly" without specifics | Be precise: "made from organic bamboo," "plastic-free packaging" |
| Use low-res photography | Minimum 2,000px shortest side for digital; 300dpi for print |
| Use ALL CAPS in body copy | Use sentence case throughout |
| Use cold-toned photography | Warm color temperature only |
| Use stock photography clichés | Real people, real homes, real moments |
| Mix illustration styles | Botanical line drawings only |
